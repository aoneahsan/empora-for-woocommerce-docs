The official route: each job is a separate paid extension from the WooCommerce marketplace, each with its own yearly licence.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** <a href="https://woocommerce.com/products/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Check their site yourself</a>. The side-by-side table is on the [Empora site](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/compare/woocommerce-marketplace-extensions).

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for WooCommerce.com extensions (bought one by one) settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in WooCommerce.com extensions (bought one by one): the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate WooCommerce.com extensions (bought one by one) on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When WooCommerce.com extensions (bought one by one) is the better choice

- A store that wants the extension WooCommerce itself maintains, with its own support channel.
- A store that needs only one or two of these jobs: one extension costs less than any paid Empora plan that includes it.
