# AI Drafts in Your Empora Account

**AI drafts** is a page in your Empora account at
[empora.aoneahsan.com/dashboard/ai](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/dashboard/ai), after Stores. Draft a product
description, or ask how a module's settings work. You read every word before it goes anywhere.

## What you can ask for

| Task | What you write | What comes back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product description** | Notes about the product: materials, sizes, colours, who it is for. The draft uses only what you write here. | One short paragraph and three to five points, in at most 180 words. |
| **Help with module settings** | Your question. Name the module and say what you want it to do. | Short steps, in at most 200 words. |

The box holds 4,000 characters. Press **Generate draft**, then **Copy** to paste the draft wherever you need it.
**Try again** writes a new draft from the same text and uses one more draft.

Read a product description before you use it: check every size, material and claim against the product. Check
each settings step against the module's own settings screen before you change anything.

## Speak

**Speak** turns your voice into text with your browser's own speech recognition, which may send the audio to the
browser's maker (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari, Microsoft in Edge). Your words appear in the box, and you can
edit them before you generate. Speech input needs a browser with speech recognition, such as Chrome, Edge or
Safari. You can type instead.

## Your allowance and level

**This month** shows how many drafts are left on your plan and when they start again. The level beside it says
how the drafts are written: **Spark** (fast and efficient), **Pulse** (sharper reasoning) or **Surge** (deepest
thinking). When this month's drafts are used, or AI drafts are not part of your plan, the page links to the
[pricing page](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/pricing), which shows how many drafts each plan gives you a month.

## Your text

Your text goes through Empora's server to an AI service that writes the draft. Empora counts the draft and does
not keep your text or the draft.
