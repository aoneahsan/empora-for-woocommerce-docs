Your Empora account has a new page, **AI drafts**, after Stores.

<!-- truncate -->

Draft a product description, or ask how a module's settings work. You read every word before it goes anywhere.

- **Product description** turns rough notes into a description you can paste into WooCommerce.
- **Help with module settings** answers how to set up one of Empora's modules, in short steps.
- **Speak** fills the box from your voice, through your browser's own speech recognition.

Each plan includes a number of drafts a month, shown at the top of the page. Details:
[AI Drafts in Your Empora Account](/getting-started/ai-drafts).
