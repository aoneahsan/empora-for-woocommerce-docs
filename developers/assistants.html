Assistants

Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code and Cursor can look after your Empora licences and store connections for you. The assistant asks Empora for permission first, you sign in and choose what it can reach, and it then works through one address:

https://empora-api.aoneahsan.com/mcp

That is an MCP server, the standard way these assistants reach outside services. It reaches the same four parts of your account as an access key, and nothing on your store.

Connecting​

The same steps, with the address above to copy, are in your account under Settings, Other apps, Connected assistants.

Claude​

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Empora and paste the address above.
  3. Claude opens Empora in a new window. Sign in, read what it asks for, and approve.

ChatGPT​

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Connectors, and add a new connector.
  2. Name it Empora, paste the address above and choose OAuth to sign in.
  3. ChatGPT opens Empora. Sign in, read what it asks for, and approve.

Claude Code​

  1. Run this in your terminal:

    claude mcp add --transport http empora https://empora-api.aoneahsan.com/mcp

  2. Start Claude Code, type /mcp and choose empora to sign in.

  3. Your browser opens Empora. Sign in, read what it asks for, and approve.

Cursor​

  1. In Cursor, open Settings, then MCP, and add a new server. Paste this into the file it opens:

    { "mcpServers": { "empora": { "url": "https://empora-api.aoneahsan.com/mcp" } } }

  2. Save the file. Cursor shows Empora with a button to sign in.

  3. Your browser opens Empora. Sign in, read what it asks for, and approve.

The menu names inside each assistant belong to its maker and can move between their releases. The address does not change.

What you approve​

The connect page says which assistant is asking, which address it sends you back to when you finish, and which Google account you are connecting. With two-factor on, it opens only once your sign-in has passed it.

Then it lists the four parts, each with Read, Change and Remove where they apply. Take off any tick you do not want. Remove is unticked unless you tick it, whatever the assistant asked for. Underneath, the page lists what is never shared, whatever you tick: your licence keys, the WooCommerce keys behind a store connection, your shoppers and orders, card details and your Google sign-in.

An assistant that registered itself gives itself its name, and Empora has not checked who made it. The page says so in that case: approve only an assistant you added yourself, just now. A connect request lasts 10 minutes; after that, start again from the assistant.

Approve connects it and sends you back to the assistant. Deny shares nothing and tells the assistant you said no. Either way you can close the window. After an approval, Empora emails you with the assistant's name, the address it sends you to and what it can do.

What an assistant can do​

An assistant sees only the tools its ticks allow.

ToolNeedsWhat it does
whoamiNothingShows your name, your plan, what this connection may do and today's use of your plan's limits
list_licencesLicences, ReadLists your licences with their plan, status, expiry date and how many sites each allows
get_licenceLicences, ReadShows one licence by its id
list_sitesSites, ReadLists the sites your licences are activated on, with their WordPress, WooCommerce and Empora versions
deactivate_siteSites, ChangeDeactivates one site on one of your licences, which frees its place on the licence
get_planPlan and billing, ReadShows your plan, its status and the next renewal date
list_storesStore connections, ReadLists your store connections with their address, last sync and the totals shown
get_storeStore connections, ReadShows one store connection by its id
rename_storeStore connections, ChangeRenames one store connection. Only the name on Empora changes
prepare_disconnect_storeStore connections, RemoveAsks to disconnect a store. Nothing changes until you say yes on Empora within 10 minutes
get_removal_requestNothingShows whether a disconnect this connection asked for was confirmed, kept or expired

A list gives at most 50 results at a time. An assistant can make at most 10 changes a minute, and every tool call counts against your plan's requests a day, the same count your access keys use.

Disconnecting a store waits for your yes​

When you ask an assistant to disconnect a store, it can only ask Empora. Empora answers with a link to https://empora.aoneahsan.com/dashboard/confirm/…, which the assistant passes to you. Open it signed in, read what goes and what stays, and choose Disconnect or Keep it connected. The assistant cannot press either for you.

The request lasts 10 minutes, then expires with nothing changed. A waiting request also appears at the top of Other apps, as "One request is waiting for your yes". The full list of what goes and what stays is in Removals wait for you.

Renaming and disconnecting​

Each connected assistant has a row in Connected assistants, with the date it was connected and the address it sends you to.

  • Rename changes the name you see in Empora. Only you see this name; the assistant keeps its own.
  • Disconnect ends the connection at once. Empora checks the connection on every call, so the assistant's next request gets a 401 answer, even one sent a second later. To use it again, connect it again.

An assistant stays signed in by renewing its own sign-in in the background. If it goes 30 days without doing that, it has to connect again.

How many you can connect​

Free allows 3 assistant connections, Starter, Professional and Business allow 10, and Enterprise allows 25. Disconnecting one frees its place. Connecting the same assistant again replaces its old connection instead of taking a second place. When all are in use, the connect page says so before you approve.

The assistant reads your own words as data​

Your store names and the notes on your account reach the assistant as plain data. Empora never passes on an instruction written inside them, and no tool lets one connection reach another person's account. An assistant can still misread what it is shown, so read what it proposes before you say yes to a change.

ItemValue
TransportStreamable HTTP, stateless. POST /mcp only; GET and DELETE answer 405
Protocol versions2026-07-28, 2025-11-25, 2025-06-18, 2025-03-26

Client registrationDynamic registration at /oauth/register (RFC 7591). Registering grants nothing
Resourceresource must be https://empora-api.aoneahsan.com/mcp
ScopesThe seven scopes in Access keys, plus offline_access to stay connected
Browser originsA request carrying an Origin header must come from https://claude.ai, https://chatgpt.com or https://chat.openai.com. Server-to-server calls send none
SizesA request body up to 64 KB; a tool result up to 32 KB
