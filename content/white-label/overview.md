# White-label & licensing

A white-label licence means we run Empora for you with your name, logo and colours on it. You offer it to your
own merchants; we host it, deploy it and keep it updated. This section explains what that covers. Prices are not
repeated here: the [pricing page](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing) reads them from our licence
service, and it is the figure you pay. Payment is by Wise bank transfer; we confirm the transfer and email
your licence key.

## Two ways to buy

- **A licence.** Not exclusive: we may license Empora to others as well. You pick the parts you want under your
  brand, and two parts together cost less than the two apart.
- **The buyout.** Exclusive. You receive the private source code of the dashboard and its backend, and we stop
  selling Empora to anyone else. You then pay us a 7% royalty on the gross revenue Empora earns, for as long as
  it earns. Details: [the buyout](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).

## What stays GPL

Empora for WooCommerce, free and premium, is released under the GPL, version 2 or later. Its source comes with
every copy, including the one we build under your brand, and the GPL governs what you may do with it. A licence
adds what the GPL does not cover: the hosted dashboard, the update channel and the right to the Empora name and
logo. A buyout does not sell the plugin a second time; you receive it with the rest, under the same licence.
