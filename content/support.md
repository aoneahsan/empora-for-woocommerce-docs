# Support & contact

## Before you write

Most reports are resolved faster if you check three things first. They take about a minute between them:

1. **[Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting)** covers the common failures — a module that will not enable, a
   license that will not activate, a filter that returns nothing.
2. **Deactivate every other plugin** and re-test. A conflict with another extension is the single most common
   cause, and knowing whether the problem survives a clean slate halves the diagnosis.
3. **Check your versions** against [Requirements](/reference/requirements). WordPress 6.2, WooCommerce 8.0 and
   PHP 8.1 are floors, not suggestions.

Empora is not yet listed on WordPress.org, so there is no WordPress.org support forum for it. The contact form
above is the channel.

## What to include

A report with these six things can usually be answered in one reply. A report without them takes three.

- **What you did**, in the order you did it.
- **What happened**, and what you expected instead.
- **The module key** — the short identifier under the module name in
  [the module reference](/modules/reference), such as `product_filters` or `gift_cards`.
- **Versions**: WordPress, WooCommerce, PHP, and Empora. All four are on **WooCommerce → Status**.
- **Any error text**, copied rather than described, including anything in your `debug.log`.
- **Whether it survives deactivating other plugins.**

Screenshots help for anything visual. For a checkout or pricing problem, the product and the exact cart
contents that trigger it are worth more than a screenshot.

## Reporting a security issue

Do not open a public report for a security problem. Write to the contact form above with the details and give
it time to be fixed before you disclose it anywhere. A security report is answered ahead of everything else.

## Response times

Empora is built and maintained by one developer, [Ahsan Mahmood](/about-the-author). Replies are usually
within a couple of working days. There is no support SLA on any plan today; if you need a contractual
response time, say so when you write and it can be discussed.
