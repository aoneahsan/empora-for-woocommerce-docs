Every page of your Empora account now has a page tour.

<!-- truncate -->

## Three new tours

- **Downloads** shows the free edition, the premium download for each licence, how to install it, and what
  Empora holds.
- **AI drafts** shows how many drafts your plan leaves you this month, where to ask for a draft, and the
  Generate draft button.
- **Referrals** shows your referral link, how close you are to the next free plan month, and your sign-ups.

Start one with the map button in the header, labelled **Take a tour of this page**.

## Smaller changes

- An expired licence now carries an **Expired** tag in Billing → Licences.

Details: [Your Empora Account and Page Tours](/getting-started/your-empora-account).
