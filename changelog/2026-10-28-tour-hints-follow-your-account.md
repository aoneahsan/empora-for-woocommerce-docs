The page tours now remember you, not just the device you used.

<!-- truncate -->

## One offer per page, on every device

The first time you open Overview, License, Stores, Billing or Settings, one line offers that page's tour. Once
your account has seen it, it counts as seen on every device you sign in on. The map button in the header still
starts the tour whenever you like.

## How Empora works, from the home page

The home page now has a third button, **See how Empora works**, after **Get started** and **Explore
features**. It opens [How Empora works](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/how-empora-works), six screens of the
account, one step at a time, before you sign in.

Details: [Your Empora Account and Page Tours](/getting-started/your-empora-account).
