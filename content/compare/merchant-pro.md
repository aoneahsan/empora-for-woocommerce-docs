A conversion-focused module plugin from aThemes, described on its site as 40 or more WooCommerce tools in one plugin.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** <a href="https://athemes.com/merchant/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Check their site yourself</a>. The side-by-side table is on the [Empora site](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/compare/merchant-pro).

## What it costs there

- **Merchant Pro, Personal (1 website): $79 a year.** 1 website. The page prints $79 and $99 under Annual and Lifetime without pairing them. <a href="https://athemes.com/merchant/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **Merchant Pro, Professional (10 websites): $159 a year.** 10 websites. The page prints $159 and $199 the same way. <a href="https://athemes.com/merchant/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **Merchant Pro, Agency (100 websites): $319 a year.** 100 websites. The page prints $319 and $399 the same way. <a href="https://athemes.com/merchant/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for Merchant Pro (aThemes) settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in Merchant Pro (aThemes): the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate Merchant Pro (aThemes) on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When Merchant Pro (aThemes) is the better choice

- A store that wants storefront persuasion tools: stock scarcity, cart reserved timers, recent sales notifications, side cart and added-to-cart popups are all named modules there and are not Empora modules.
- A store with many sites: the Agency plan states 100 websites.
