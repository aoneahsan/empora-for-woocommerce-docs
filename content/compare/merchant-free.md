## Where each job goes in Empora

The Merchant (free, WordPress.org) pages we read state none of the jobs on our comparison table, so check what you use against the [module reference](/modules/reference).

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for Merchant (free, WordPress.org) settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in Merchant (free, WordPress.org): the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate Merchant (free, WordPress.org) on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When Merchant (free, WordPress.org) is the better choice

- A store that wants free, published storefront touches today: product labels, quick view, buy now, pre-orders and trust badges are in the free 16, and it is installable from WordPress.org now.
- A store that does not want a licence key at all.
