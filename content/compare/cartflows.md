## What it costs there

- **CartFlows Starter: $99 a year.** 1 website. Renews at $149 a year. <a href="https://cartflows.com/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **CartFlows Pro: $199 a year.** 3 websites. Renews at $299 a year; also $699 one time. <a href="https://cartflows.com/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **CartFlows Suite: $299 a year.** 30 websites, 6 plugins. Renews at $449 a year; also $999 one time. <a href="https://cartflows.com/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for CartFlows settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in CartFlows: the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate CartFlows on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When CartFlows is the better choice

- A store that lives on landing pages and sales funnels: the page lists 15 funnel features with checkout, order bumps, upsells and A/B testing as the core product.
- A store that prefers a one-time payment: Pro and Suite each state a one-time price beside the yearly one.
