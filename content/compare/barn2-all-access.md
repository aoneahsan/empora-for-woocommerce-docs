## Moving across

Empora has no importer for Barn2 All Access Pass settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in Barn2 All Access Pass: the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate Barn2 All Access Pass on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When Barn2 All Access Pass is the better choice

- A store that sells from a product table or takes restaurant orders: those are Barn2's own plugins and Empora has no product-table module.
- A store that wants a discount manager and product filters from a vendor whose pass states a fixed 19-plugin list in USD.
