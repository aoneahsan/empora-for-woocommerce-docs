## Moving across

Empora has no importer for WebToffee bundles settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in WebToffee bundles: the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate WebToffee bundles on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When WebToffee bundles is the better choice

- A store whose jobs are paperwork: invoices, packing slips, credit notes, shipping labels, picklists and sequential order numbers are each a dedicated plugin there.
- A store that wants only coupons and gift cards for under $100 a year.
