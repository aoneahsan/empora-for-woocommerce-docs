Each page below says what the other product costs, where each job goes in Empora, the steps for moving across, and when the other product is the better choice.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** Prices and features come from each vendor's own pages, and every one links to where we read it. The full comparison is on the [Empora site](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/compare).

- [Booster for WooCommerce](/compare/booster-for-woocommerce): A long-running all-in-one plugin with a free core on WordPress.org and an Elite tier that the vendor counts at 140 or more modules.
- [WooCommerce.com extensions (bought one by one)](/compare/woocommerce-marketplace-extensions): The official route: each job is a separate paid extension from the WooCommerce marketplace, each with its own yearly licence.
- [FunnelKit](/compare/funnelkit): A checkout, funnel and marketing-automation suite for WooCommerce, sold as yearly plans by site count.
- [CartFlows](/compare/cartflows): A funnel builder for WooCommerce whose top plan bundles a cart-abandonment, cart and coupons plugin alongside it.
- [Merchant Pro (aThemes)](/compare/merchant-pro): A conversion-focused module plugin from aThemes, described on its site as 40 or more WooCommerce tools in one plugin.
- [ShopEngine (Wpmet)](/compare/shopengine): A WooCommerce builder and module plugin for Elementor from Wpmet, with a free tier and yearly or lifetime licences.
- [WooLentor (ShopLentor)](/compare/woolentor): A WooCommerce module and page-builder plugin with a free tier and a Pro tier sold by site count.
- [WebToffee bundles](/compare/webtoffee-bundles): A plugin vendor whose bundle packs group its invoicing, coupon, gift-card and import-export plugins at a discount; each plugin keeps a single-site licence.
- [StoreApps Smart Coupons](/compare/storeapps-smart-coupons): StoreApps sells each plugin on its own; Smart Coupons is the one a store buys for gift cards, store credit and buy-one-get-one offers.
- [Sparks for WooCommerce (Themeisle)](/compare/sparks-for-woocommerce): A storefront-feature plugin from Themeisle: wishlist, comparison tables, quick view, swatches, product tabs and announcement bars, sold yearly.
- [Barn2 All Access Pass](/compare/barn2-all-access): One yearly pass to every Barn2 plugin (19 listed), including product tables, filters, quick view, discounts and restaurant ordering.
- [WPSwings bundle](/compare/wpswings-bundle): A plugin vendor that sells subscriptions, gift cards, points, wallets, RMA and upsell funnels as separate Pro plugins and groups them into offer bundles.
- [YITH plugins (bought one by one)](/compare/yith-plugins): A large catalogue of single-job WooCommerce plugins, each licensed for a year; the YITH Club bundle is closed to new customers by the vendor's own announcement.
- [Merchant (free, WordPress.org)](/compare/merchant-free): The free option on this table: aThemes' Merchant with 16 modules at no cost, published on WordPress.org.
