# What you get, part by part

A licence covers the parts on your order. Empora ships two today.

## The WordPress plugin

Empora for WooCommerce, built under your name. Your merchants install it on their own WordPress sites, it carries
your brand in wp-admin, and its updates are served from your dashboard rather than ours. Every module the plugin
has today is in it; a merchant's plan on your dashboard decides which ones they can switch on. The plugin's source
comes with it under the GPL.

## The dashboard web app

The account dashboard your merchants sign in to, on a domain you choose: their licences, connected stores,
downloads, billing and AI drafts. We host it and run it. Its source code is not handed over with a licence; it
changes hands only with a buyout.

## What is not part of a licence yet

The Android app is built but has not been released, so no licence includes it today. When it is, it joins as a
third part; an iOS build would then need an Apple developer account that you provide or fund.

## Costs that stay yours

Your domain, store accounts and any paid services you choose to connect are yours to pay for.

See the parts and their prices on the [pricing page](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing).
