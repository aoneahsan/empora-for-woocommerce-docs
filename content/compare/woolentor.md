- **Plus (5 websites): $99 a year.** 5 websites, 1 year of updates. <a href="https://woolentor.com/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **Growth (100 websites): $199 a year.** 100 websites, 1 year of updates. <a href="https://woolentor.com/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **Growth One Time (100 websites): $399 one time.** 100 websites, lifetime updates. <a href="https://woolentor.com/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for WooLentor (ShopLentor) settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in WooLentor (ShopLentor): the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate WooLentor (ShopLentor) on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When WooLentor (ShopLentor) is the better choice

- A store that wants a free wishlist, compare, backorder and flash-sale countdown without paying: the free column on their pricing page lists them.
- A store with many sites on a small budget: 5 websites for the lowest yearly tier, 100 for the next.
