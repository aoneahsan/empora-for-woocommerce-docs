# Plans & pricing

Empora has one free tier and four paid plans. The free tier needs no license and no account; the paid plans
unlock progressively more of the 78 modules, and each one includes everything below it.

<PlanTable />

Prices are per month, in US dollars. This table is generated from the product's own plan catalog — the same
file the plugin reads to decide what your license unlocks — so it cannot drift from what the software
actually enforces.

## Self-serve purchase is not available yet

**Version 1.0 ships without a payment provider.** There is no checkout, no card form and no way to buy a plan
from the site today. The plans above are real, the limits above are enforced by the software, and a paid plan
is granted directly on your account — but the purchase step is arranged by contacting the author rather than
by paying online.

To arrange a paid plan, [get in touch](/support). Say which plan you want and how many sites you run, and
you will get a licence key back.

The free tier is unaffected: it requires no license, no payment and no contact with anyone.

## What the free tier includes

Seven modules, permanently, with no license:

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| Product Filters | AJAX filtering by price, attribute, category, tag and rating |
| Reviews Enhancement | Richer review display with verified-purchase indicators |
| Wishlist | Customer-saved product lists |
| Product Compare | Side-by-side product comparison |
| Social Media Integration | Share buttons and social meta tags |
| SEO & Schema Markup | Product schema and meta tag output |
| Dynamic Pricing | Quantity discounts and percentage-off rules |

The free tier is capped at 100 products, 500 orders and 100 customers, and does not include the import or
export tools. Those caps apply to what Empora's own modules process — they are not caps on your WooCommerce
store, which keeps working exactly as it did.

## How the limits work

- **Products, orders, customers** — the ceiling on how many records Empora's modules will process on a plan.
  `Unlimited` means no ceiling is applied.
- **Imports and exports per month** — how many bulk import or export runs the plan allows. `Not included`
  means the import/export module is not part of that plan.
- **Sites** — how many separate WordPress installations one license may be activated on. Deactivating a site
  frees its slot.

## Choosing a plan

- **Free** — you want better filtering, a wishlist and product comparison on a small store, and nothing else.
- **Starter** — one store, and you want the storefront and pricing modules rather than the operational ones.
- **Professional** — one store with real back-office needs: imports and exports, more modules, higher ceilings.
- **Business** — up to three stores, no record ceilings, and the large majority of the catalogue.
- **Enterprise** — unlimited sites and every module, including the specialist ones.

If you are between two plans, start lower. Plans are changed on an existing license without reinstalling
anything, and your settings and data are untouched by a plan change.

:::info Plan contents may still change
Empora 1.0 has not been released yet. Which modules sit in which paid plan is settled in the catalog above,
but it can still be revised before general availability. The free tier's seven modules are fixed.
:::
