A WooCommerce builder and module plugin for Elementor from Wpmet, with a free tier and yearly or lifetime licences.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** <a href="https://wpmet.com/plugin/shopengine/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Check their site yourself</a>. The side-by-side table is on the [Empora site](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/compare/shopengine).

- **Single (1 site): $54 a year.** 1 site, 1 year of updates. The page also prints $59. <a href="https://wpmet.com/plugin/shopengine/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **5 Sites: $120 a year.** 5 sites, 1 year of updates. The page also prints $149. <a href="https://wpmet.com/plugin/shopengine/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **Unlimited: $210 a year.** Unlimited sites, 1 year of updates. The page also prints $299. <a href="https://wpmet.com/plugin/shopengine/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **Single - Lifetime: $135 one time.** 1 site, buy once. The page also prints $149. <a href="https://wpmet.com/plugin/shopengine/pricing/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for ShopEngine (Wpmet) settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in ShopEngine (Wpmet): the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate ShopEngine (Wpmet) on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When ShopEngine (Wpmet) is the better choice

- A store built in Elementor that wants to design its shop, cart and checkout templates in the same tool as its modules.
- A store that wants a lifetime licence: every tier has a buy-once price on the page.
