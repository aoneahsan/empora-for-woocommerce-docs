Your Empora Account and Page Tours

Your Empora account at empora.aoneahsan.com holds your licenses, downloads, connected stores, billing and settings. You sign in with Google.

See it before you sign in​

On the home page, See how Empora works sits beside Get started and Explore features. How Empora works shows six screens of the account, one step at a time. Use the numbered dots, Previous and Next, the arrow keys, or swipe on a phone. Each step has its own address (?step=3), so you can share a single step.

On a phone​

On a phone, the account works like an app. The page title sits at the top with one action beside it, and a bar along the bottom holds Overview, Licences, Stores, AI drafts and More. More holds Downloads, Billing, Referrals and Settings, as well as How Empora works, notifications, appearance settings and signing out. Each page's main action sits just above the bar, within reach of your thumb, and dialogs open as sheets from the bottom of the screen.

A tour on every page​

Overview, License, Downloads, Stores, AI drafts, Billing, Referrals and Settings each have a map button in the header, labelled Take a tour of this page. It walks you through the page you are on:

  • Each step highlights one area and explains it in a card.
  • All steps lets you jump to any step. Skip tour and Close end it at once.
  • The arrow keys move between steps, and Escape closes the tour.
  • On a phone the card opens as a sheet at the bottom of the screen, with steps written for the phone layout.
  • A tour can open on a given step from its address (?tour=3).

The first time you open one of these pages, one line offers the tour: Show me starts it and Dismiss hides it. The line shows once for your account: a page you have seen counts as seen on every device you sign in on. It leaves by itself after a few seconds, and the map button starts the tour again at any time.

What each page does​

PageWhat it is for
OverviewRevenue, orders and customers from every connected store, added up in one place. Sync every store from one button, or one store at a time.
LicenseReveal the key and copy it into Empora on your WordPress site. Every activated site is listed, and staging sites count towards your allowance. See Activating Your License.
StoresEnter the store address; the Empora for WooCommerce plugin must be active on it. Each store shows its orders, products and customers, and when it last synced. Removing a store from the dashboard leaves the store itself untouched.
BillingThe modules and limits your plan includes, and the date it renews. It also shows what stays in your WordPress database, and what Empora holds.
SettingsYour profile and security. Sign this device out from Settings; your other devices stay signed in.
