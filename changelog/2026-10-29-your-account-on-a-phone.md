Every page of your Empora account has a new look, and on a phone the account now works like an app.

<!-- truncate -->

## A bar for the pages you use most

On a phone, a bar along the bottom of the screen holds **Overview**, **Licences**, **Stores**, **AI drafts** and
**More**. **More** holds Downloads, Billing, Referrals and Settings, as well as How Empora works, notifications,
appearance settings and signing out.

## Actions within reach

Each page's main action, such as **Sync every store** or **Generate draft**, sits just above the bar. Dialogs
open as sheets from the bottom of the screen, and the page tours run on the phone layout too.

Details: [Your Empora Account and Page Tours](/getting-started/your-empora-account).
