## Moving across

Empora has no importer for Booster for WooCommerce settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in Booster for WooCommerce: the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate Booster for WooCommerce on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When Booster for WooCommerce is the better choice

- A store that wants a free all-in-one toolkit from WordPress.org today: Booster's free core is listed there, and Empora's free core is not on WordPress.org yet.
- A store that counts modules: the vendor states 140 or more in Elite against Empora's 82.
