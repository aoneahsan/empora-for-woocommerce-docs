StoreApps sells each plugin on its own; Smart Coupons is the one a store buys for gift cards, store credit and buy-one-get-one offers.

**Last checked 2026-11-07.** <a href="https://www.storeapps.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Check their site yourself</a>. The side-by-side table is on the [Empora site](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/compare/storeapps-smart-coupons).

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for StoreApps Smart Coupons settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in StoreApps Smart Coupons: the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate StoreApps Smart Coupons on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When StoreApps Smart Coupons is the better choice

- A store that wants a mature coupon engine: scheduled gift cards, store credit as a refund alternative, URL coupons and buy-X-get-Y variants are all described on the product page.
- A store that needs exactly one of these jobs for $99 a year on one site.
