## Moving across

Empora has no importer for WPSwings bundle settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in WPSwings bundle: the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate WPSwings bundle on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When WPSwings bundle is the better choice

- A store that wants returns and refunds, a wallet, membership and event tickets from the same vendor, each as a dedicated plugin with its own page.
- A store that prefers to pick two plugins at a time: the offers page prices named pairs.
