# About the Empora team

**Empora for WooCommerce** is made by the Empora team: one plugin with 82 WooCommerce modules that runs on your
own WordPress install, sold through a dashboard where you manage licences and plans.

## Reach the team

- Email: [hi@aoneahsan.com](mailto:hi@aoneahsan.com)
- The [support page](/support) has the contact form and what to include in a report.

## The product

- Product and dashboard: [empora.aoneahsan.com](https://empora.aoneahsan.com)
- Documentation: [empora-docs.aoneahsan.com](https://empora-docs.aoneahsan.com)
- These docs (source): [github.com/aoneahsan/empora-for-woocommerce-docs](https://github.com/aoneahsan/empora-for-woocommerce-docs)

## Support the work

If Empora saves you time, you can support its development at [aoneahsan.com/payment](https://aoneahsan.com/payment).
Support is always optional, and the free modules stay free.

## Contributing to the docs

These pages are open source. Spotted a gap or an error? Open an issue or a pull request on the
[docs repository](https://github.com/aoneahsan/empora-for-woocommerce-docs). Each page has an "Edit this page"
link at the bottom.
