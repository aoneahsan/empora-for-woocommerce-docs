# Your Empora Account and Page Tours

Your Empora account at [empora.aoneahsan.com](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/dashboard) holds your licenses,
downloads, connected stores, billing and settings. You sign in with Google.

## See it before you sign in

On the home page, **See how Empora works** sits beside **Get started** and **Explore features**.
[How Empora works](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/how-empora-works) shows six screens of the account, one step
at a time. Use the numbered dots, **Previous** and **Next**, the arrow keys, or swipe on a phone. Each step has
its own address (`?step=3`), so you can share a single step.

## A tour on every page

Overview, License, Stores, Billing and Settings each have a **map button** in the header, labelled **Take a
tour of this page**. It walks you through the page you are on:

- Each step highlights one area and explains it in a card.
- **All steps** lets you jump to any step. **Skip tour** and **Close** end it at once.
- The arrow keys move between steps, and **Escape** closes the tour.
- On a phone the card opens as a sheet at the bottom of the screen, with steps written for the phone layout.
- A tour can open on a given step from its address (`?tour=3`).

The first time you open one of these pages, one line offers the tour: **Show me** starts it and **Dismiss**
hides it. The line shows once for your account: a page you have seen counts as seen on every device you sign
in on. It leaves by itself after a few seconds, and the map button starts the tour again at any time.

## What each page does

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Revenue, orders and customers from every connected store, added up in one place. Sync every store from one button, or one store at a time. |
| **License** | Reveal the key and copy it into Empora on your WordPress site. Every activated site is listed, and staging sites count towards your allowance. See [Activating Your License](./activating-your-license). |
| **Stores** | Enter the store address; the Empora for WooCommerce plugin must be active on it. Each store shows its orders, products and customers, and when it last synced. Removing a store from the dashboard leaves the store itself untouched. |
| **Billing** | The modules and limits your plan includes, and the date it renews. It also shows what stays in your WordPress database, and what Empora holds. |
| **Settings** | Your profile and security. Sign this device out from Settings; your other devices stay signed in. |
