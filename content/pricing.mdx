# Plans & pricing

Empora has one free tier and four paid plans. The free tier needs no license and no account; the paid plans
unlock progressively more of the 82 modules, and each one includes everything below it.

<PlanTable />

Prices are per month, in US dollars. This table is generated from the product's own plan catalog — the same
file the plugin reads to decide what your license unlocks — so it cannot drift from what the software
actually enforces.

## Self-serve purchase is not available yet

**Version 1.0 ships without a payment provider.** There is no checkout, no card form and no way to buy a plan
from the site today. The plans above are real, the limits above are enforced by the software, and a paid plan
is granted directly on your account — but the purchase step is arranged by contacting the author rather than
by paying online.

To arrange a paid plan, [get in touch](/support). Say which plan you want and how many sites you run, and
you will get a licence key back.

The free tier is unaffected: it requires no license, no payment and no contact with anyone.

## What the free tier includes

Eight modules, permanently, with no license:

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| Product Filters | AJAX filtering by price, attribute, category, tag and rating |
| Reviews Enhancement | Richer review display with verified-purchase indicators |
| Wishlist | Customer-saved product lists |
| Product Import/Export | Products in and out as CSV or XML, with the core fields |
| Product Compare | Side-by-side product comparison |
| Social Media Integration | Share buttons and social meta tags |
| SEO & Schema Markup | Product schema and meta tag output |
| Dynamic Pricing | Quantity discounts and percentage-off rules |

The free tier does not cap how many products, orders or customers Empora's modules will process — a row in
your own database costs us nothing. Import/Export on the free tier moves the core of each product: name, SKU,
type, prices, descriptions, categories, stock and status. Nothing here caps your WooCommerce store, which keeps
working exactly as it did.

## How the limits work

- **Products, orders, customers** — how many records Empora's modules will process on a plan. Every tier,
  free included, reads `Unlimited`: no ceiling is applied to record counts on any plan.
- **Import/Export** — what an import or export carries. Free moves the core fields; every paid plan moves every
  field, with variations, attributes, images, linked products, downloads and custom fields. No plan caps how
  many runs you make.
- **Sites** — how many separate WordPress installations one license may be activated on. Deactivating a site
  frees its slot.

## Choosing a plan

- **Free** — you want better filtering, a wishlist and product comparison on a small store, and nothing else.
- **Starter** — one store, and you want the storefront and pricing modules rather than the operational ones.
- **Professional** — one store with real back-office needs, and more modules.
- **Business** — up to three stores and 71 of the 82 modules.
- **Enterprise** — unlimited sites and every module, including the specialist ones.

If you are between two plans, start lower. Plans are changed on an existing license without reinstalling
anything, and your settings and data are untouched by a plan change.

:::info Plan contents may still change
Empora 1.0 has not been released yet. Which modules sit in which paid plan is settled in the catalog above,
but it can still be revised before general availability. The free tier's eight modules are fixed.
:::

## Referrals

Your account on [empora.aoneahsan.com](https://empora.aoneahsan.com) has a **Referrals** page with your
own invite link. When a friend creates an Empora account through it, you earn points and a badge, and enough
confirmed friends earn you months of a paid plan. The page shows the current numbers and your history.

This is about Empora accounts. The [Referral Program](/modules/reference/referral-program) module is a
separate thing: it runs a referral scheme for your own store's customers.

## White-label licence and buyout

Agencies, hosts and platforms can run Empora under their own brand, or buy its private source outright. That is
priced separately from the plans above, and the figures live on one page only, the
[white-label pricing page](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them from our licence
service. What a licence covers is in [White-label & licensing](/white-label).
