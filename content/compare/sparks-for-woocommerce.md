## Moving across

Empora has no importer for Sparks for WooCommerce (Themeisle) settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in Sparks for WooCommerce (Themeisle): the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate Sparks for WooCommerce (Themeisle) on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When Sparks for WooCommerce (Themeisle) is the better choice

- A store that wants a small, focused set of product-page features at the lowest yearly price on this table.
- A store already on a Themeisle theme such as Neve, where the same vendor ships both.
