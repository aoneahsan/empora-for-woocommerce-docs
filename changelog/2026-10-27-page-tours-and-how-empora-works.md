Your Empora account now shows you around itself.

<!-- truncate -->

## A tour on every page

Overview, License, Stores, Billing and Settings each have a map button in the header. It walks you through the
page you are on, one highlighted area at a time. You can skip it, close it or jump to any step, and the button
starts it again whenever you like. On a phone the steps open in a sheet at the bottom of the screen.

## How Empora works

[How Empora works](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/how-empora-works) shows six screens of the account, one step
at a time, before you sign in. It ends on **Open your dashboard**.

Details: [Your Empora Account and Page Tours](/getting-started/your-empora-account).
