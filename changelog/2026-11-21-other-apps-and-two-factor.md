Settings in your Empora account has a new tab, **Other apps**, and **Security** now holds two-factor sign-in.

<!-- truncate -->

**Other apps** lets a script of yours, an assistant or a server of yours work with your licences, site activations,
plan and store connections. Each one gets only what you tick, and only from your own account. None of them reaches
your shoppers, orders or products, which stay on your store.

- **Access keys** for your own scripts and spreadsheets, calling the Empora API.
- **Connected assistants**: Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code and Cursor. Disconnecting a store always waits for your
  yes on Empora, whichever app asks for it.
- **Webhooks**: a signed message to an address of yours when a licence, site, plan or connection changes.

**Security** turns on two-factor sign-in: a code from your phone after Google, with ten recovery codes in case the
phone is lost. It is off unless you turn it on.

Every plan includes all of this, Free too, with limits on each plan. Details:
[Empora for developers](/developers).
