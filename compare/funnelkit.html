Moving from FunnelKit to Empora

A checkout, funnel and marketing-automation suite for WooCommerce, sold as yearly plans by site count.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Check their site yourself. The side-by-side table is on the Empora site.

What it costs there​

  • Basic: $99.50 a year. 1 site. Introductory price, normally $129; renews at full price. Where we read this.
  • Plus: $149.50 a year. 2 sites. Introductory, normally $299; renews at full price. Where we read this.
  • Professional: $199.50 a year. 3 sites. Introductory, normally $399; renews at full price. Where we read this.
  • Elite: $299.50 a year. 30 sites. Introductory, normally $599; renews at full price. Where we read this.

Empora's own plans are on the pricing page.

Moving across​

Empora has no importer for FunnelKit settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

  1. List what you use in FunnelKit: the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
  2. Install Empora on the staging copy.
  3. If a job needs a paid module, activate your licence.
  4. Switch on and configure the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
  5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
  6. Deactivate FunnelKit on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

When FunnelKit is the better choice​

  • A store whose main job is the funnel: one-click upsells with a rule engine, A/B testing and a visual automation builder are listed on their pricing page and go deeper than Empora's upsell module.
  • A store that wants email and SMS broadcasts and 60 or more app integrations inside the same product (Professional and up).
