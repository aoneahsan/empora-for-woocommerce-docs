# Updates and renewals

An update is a new version of the plugin or the dashboard: fixes, security patches and the modules we add to
Empora. We build each one under your brand and ship it to your deployment.

- Every licence and the buyout come with a number of months of updates, counted from the day you pay. The
  [updates page](https://empora.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) shows how many, and the renewal plans.
- When the months run out, your deployment keeps running on the last version we shipped. Nothing is switched off.
- A renewal is counted from the later of today and the day your current updates end, so renewing early loses
  nothing.
- You renew with your licence key and the email the licence was issued to. The two have to match, so a key on its
  own cannot be used to find who holds it.
- Changes made only for you are custom work, not updates, and are quoted on their own.
