## What it costs there

- **YITH WooCommerce Subscription: $199.99 a year.** 1 year of updates and support. Excludes tax. <a href="https://yithemes.com/themes/plugins/yith-woocommerce-subscription/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.
- **YITH WooCommerce Wishlist (premium): $99.99 a year.** 1 year of updates and support. Excludes tax. A free version exists. <a href="https://yithemes.com/themes/plugins/yith-woocommerce-wishlist/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Where we read this</a>.

## Moving across

Empora has no importer for YITH plugins (bought one by one) settings. You enter them again, module by module, so the move is safest on a staging copy of your store.

1. List what you use in YITH plugins (bought one by one): the table above is a starting point, and your own settings screens are the full list.
2. [Install Empora](/getting-started/installation) on the staging copy.
3. If a job needs a paid module, [activate your licence](/getting-started/activating-your-license).
4. [Switch on and configure](/modules/configuring-modules) the modules for those jobs, entering your settings as you go.
5. Place a test order and check the totals, the emails and the order record.
6. Deactivate YITH plugins (bought one by one) on the staging copy and place another test order. When that is right, repeat the same steps on the live store.

## When YITH plugins (bought one by one) is the better choice

- A store that wants one deep plugin per job from a vendor with a long catalogue and a free version of the wishlist.
- A store that already runs several YITH plugins and wants them to keep matching each other.
